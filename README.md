# OFMtouch

A mobile football management game for Android. Build your squad, guide young players and shape your club's future.

## What's new in v0.6.0

- **Board:** fairer first-season league targets, youth development goals and playing-style expectations.
- **Your contract:** negotiate weekly salary, contract length and the league objective.
- **Club future:** ownership changes, takeovers and board funding for facility improvements.
- **Dressing room:** review player happiness, concerns and outstanding playing-time promises.
- **Fixes:** clearer captaincy decisions, protected promise deadlines and more realistic board pressure.

## Play your way

Choose a club, set your tactics and manage match days. Scout players, negotiate transfers and loans, develop your academy and keep the club's finances healthy. Your results and decisions shape your managerial career.

Open **Manager** to review board confidence, discuss your contract and see the dressing room. Facility improvements are also available in **Finances**. Review player conversations and transfer offers in **Inbox**.

Your launch summary shows the main changes in a few short bullets. You can hide it on future launches and read previous update notes from the main menu.
