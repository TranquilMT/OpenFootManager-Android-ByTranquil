# ⚽ OpenFootManager Android

**Build your club. Pick your team. Shape your tactics. Chase trophies.**

A free, open-source football management game being brought to Android by **TranquilMT**.

> 🚧 **Currently in development** — a fan-made Android port of the original OpenFootManager with mobile improvements, fixes and new enhancements being added over time.

## 🎮 What can you do?

- 🏟️ **Manage your own football club**
- 👥 **Build and manage your squad**
- 📋 **Pick your starting XI**
- 🧠 **Create formations and tactics**
- 🔄 **Make substitutions and match-day decisions**
- 🔎 **Scout for new players**
- 💰 **Buy and sell players**
- 📅 **Play through fixtures and competitions**
- 🏆 **Climb the table and compete for trophies**
- 📈 **Develop your players and club over multiple seasons**
- 💼 **Manage club finances and football operations**
- 💾 **Save your career and continue your journey**

## 📱 Built for Android

The Android version is being adapted for phones and tablets with:

- 👆 Touch-friendly controls
- 📱 Mobile-first menus and screens
- 🔄 Portrait and landscape support
- ⚽ Touch-friendly tactics and team selection
- 🎮 Improved match-day controls
- 🚀 Android performance and usability improvements
- ✨ Ongoing gameplay fixes, improvements and enhancements

The goal is simple: **keep the depth of OpenFootManager while making it comfortable and fun to play on Android.**

## 🚧 Current status

OpenFootManager Android is still in **alpha development**. Early builds may contain bugs or unfinished features.

The current focus is the full career loop using generated clubs and players:

**New Career → Manage Team → Tactics → Matches → Transfers → Seasons → Save & Continue**

Real-world player databases can be introduced later without holding back the playable game.

## 📦 Download builds

Development APKs are built directly from this repository. Tested milestone versions will be published through **GitHub Releases** so players can easily find the newest APK.

Current Android target: **ARM64**
