# ⚽ OFMtouch

**Your club. Your tactics. Your career. Anywhere.**

OFMtouch is a free, open-source football management game for Android, based on OpenFootManager and adapted for mobile by **TranquilMT**. Build a squad, develop young talent and lead your club through seasons of league and cup football. Every career brings decisions on the pitch, in the transfer market and behind the scenes.

**Current version: v0.7.0 — Match Engine V2 · Android ARM64**

[Download OFMtouch](https://github.com/TranquilMT/OpenFootManager-Android-ByTranquil/releases/latest) · [Previous releases](https://github.com/TranquilMT/OpenFootManager-Android-ByTranquil/releases) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/TranquilMT/OpenFootManager-Android-ByTranquil/issues/new)

- 🏟️ **Take control of a football club** and shape its identity, ambitions and future.

- 🎓 **Develop young prospects** through **Player Development V2.0**, where training, playing time and opportunity influence growth.
- 🌱 **Run an academy of up to 12 prospects**, review coach reports, recruit talent and promote players when ready.
- ⚽ **Give young players competitive opportunities** through Academy Challenge fixtures and strategic loans.
- 📅 **Play through full seasons**, following fixtures, results, league tables and the football calendar.
- 🏆 **Challenge for leagues and cups** and add named honours to your managerial history.
- 📈 **Develop players over multiple years**, from promising youngsters to first-team regulars, while managing veteran decline.
- 💼 **Manage finances and staff**, balancing recruitment ambitions against transfer and wage budgets.
- 🏗️ **Improve club facilities** and request board support for training, medical and scouting upgrades.
- 🧑‍💼 **Negotiate your manager contract**, including weekly salary, contract length and league objectives.
- 📊 **Build your manager career**, tracking club spells, wins, draws, losses and trophies.
- 🏛️ **Work with the board**, balancing league expectations, financial stability, youth development and playing style.
- 🗣️ **Manage the dressing room**, reviewing happiness, transfer requests, captaincy reactions and outstanding promises.
- 📰 **Follow club news and career stories** through Inbox updates, medical reports, board decisions and player conversations.
- 🎙️ **Enjoy expanded match commentary**, including goals, woodwork, corners and free kicks, with optional spoken commentary on supported devices.
- 🌍 **Simulate leagues across multiple regions**, with clubs across Europe, the Americas and Asia.
- 👤 **Discover a varied fictional player world**, with 20 portrait source faces, expanded nationality name pools and rare stars reaching 96 OVR.
- 💾 **Build a long-term career and football dynasty**, saving your progress and continuing season after season.

## Featured Gameplay Systems

### ⚽ Match Engine V2

Read the game with chance quality, goalkeeper saves, woodwork and recent pressure. React to formation and style changes, follow score-aware commentary and review the match after the final whistle.

### 🔄 Transfer Market V2.0

Build the right squad for your football ambitions.

- **Recruit with purpose:** clubs consider positional needs, age, ability, potential and squad-building strategy.
- **Negotiate your deals:** review incoming and outgoing offers, accept or reject terms, and respond with counter-offers.
- **Plan affordable moves:** wage space, transfer reserves and loan wage sharing influence what your club can sustain.
- **Develop through loans:** connect promising players with opportunities for competitive minutes.
- **Manage a changing market:** player willingness, first-team importance and deadline-day urgency affect recruitment.
- **Keep track of offers:** Inbox review links open the relevant player's transfer or loan offers.

### 🎓 Player Development V2.0

Create a pathway from academy prospect to first-team player.

- **Make opportunities count:** training and competitive playing time influence development.
- **Plan for potential:** age, position and development conditions shape each player's progress.
- **Build your academy:** manage up to 12 prospects separately from the senior squad, with coach reports and promotion decisions.
- **Use youth fixtures:** Academy Challenge matches need 12 available academy players and two eligible under-21 senior players.
- **Find future stars:** stronger youth facilities support intake quality, while scouting uncertainty leaves room for discovery.
- **Balance your squad:** exceptional talent remains rare, second-tier clubs can have standout players, and veterans follow different development curves.

### 💼 Manager Career, Board & Dressing Room

Your job extends beyond the next result.

- **Record your achievements:** club spells, match records and named league and cup honours follow your career.
- **Agree your terms:** negotiate manager pay, contract length and league expectations.
- **Meet broader objectives:** balance results with finances, youth development and the club's playing style.
- **Invest in the future:** request board support for facility upgrades and navigate ownership changes.
- **Build player relationships:** address happiness, playing-time concerns, contract issues and transfer requests.
- **Keep your promises:** actual minutes on the pitch matter, and captaincy choices affect the players involved.

### 📱 Football Management on Your Phone

- Touch-friendly squad, tactics, scouting, transfer, staff and finance screens.
- Responsive layouts, larger controls and support for Android display safe areas.
- Formation lists and wide information views that can be scrolled by touch.
- Matchday squads require at least 14 available players for starters and substitutes.
- Persistent match injuries with medical Inbox updates.
- Manual Save in **Settings → Saves & Data**, optional autosave and date-advance confirmation.
- Skip-to-match for moving through quiet days until football or an important decision needs attention.
- A compact What's New screen, optional launch-summary hiding and patch history from the main menu.
- Interface and update summaries in 12 supported languages.

## What's New — Version History

### v0.7.0 — Match Engine V2

**A deeper matchday: clearer chances, richer commentary and better decisions.**

- ⚽ Shared shot accuracy and conversion logic keeps live and background chance calculations aligned.
- 🎯 Recorded expected goals describe each simulated attempt before its outcome, including in-match penalties.
- 🧤 Goalkeeper-linked saves and chance-based save difficulty distinguish routine stops from demanding chances.
- 🥅 Woodwork, goalkeeper saves and expected-goals totals appear beside the action and after full time.
- 📊 The new Match Pulse shows recent attacking pressure and shots in the last five minutes.
- 🗣️ Commentary follows the score: late equalisers, late leads, comeback leads and commanding advantages.
- 🌟 Player narratives cover braces, hat-tricks and four-goal matches without counting later or same-minute goals too early or treating team-score changes as repeat goals by the scorer.
- 🔄 Substitution context distinguishes recent injuries, fresh legs, protecting a lead and chasing a result.
- 🟨 Booking risk, numerical disadvantage and injury concerns are connected to recorded match events.
- 🧠 Formation and playing-style decisions appear immediately in the match feed and retained fixture summaries.
- ⚠️ Verified defensive buildup errors, frame hits and difficult saves receive clearer event icons and prominence.
- 🏙️ Local derby labels use matching club cities and countries rather than guessing from club names.
- 🤖 Opposing managers avoid players who cannot return and weigh replacement fitness more carefully.
- 💨 Live and background midfield strength and pressing respond to current fatigue and dismissals; losing a player cannot improve a unit’s average.
- 🚑 AI managers prioritize recorded injuries and preserve reserve goalkeepers when covering outfield fatigue.
- 🧤 Emergency goalkeeper selection weighs actual available handling, reflexes and positioning.
- 🎯 Penalties share a bounded conversion model using composure, keeper ability and fatigue, including shootouts.
- 🟨 Substituted home players retain their correct booking records, and dismissed players cannot receive duplicate red-card events.
- 🧩 Formation changes and incoming substitutes retain valid roles for their actual match slots.
- 🛡️ Match configuration validates probabilities, home advantage, fatigue and clock limits before simulation.
- ⏱️ Fixed clock rewinds and missing action minutes around half-time and extra-time restarts.
- 📝 Fixture history retains new chance-quality and woodwork totals while older careers continue to load.
- 🛠️ Fixed self-assists, malformed commentary variants, unresolved placeholders and zero-strength possession calculations.
- 📱 Refreshed menu icon tiles, a major-update entry point and six compact What’s New cards support small screens and reduced motion.
- 🌍 All new interface and commentary content is translated across all 12 supported languages.

Expected goals are the engine’s simulated chance probabilities, not tracking-data measurements or guaranteed goals. Recent pressure is based on actual shot events and gives no artificial scoring bonus. Existing career, economy, transfers, academy and board systems remain part of this full release.

### v0.6.4 — Manager Progression & Planning

Build a stronger managerial career with achievements, saved milestones and clearer planning tools.

#### Career rewards

**More → Achievements** is available alongside Training and Academy, including between clubs. Ten one-time goals reward managing matches, victories, trophies, promotion, board objectives, player development, youth minutes and career longevity. Earn 1,700 XP across the full set and progress from level 1 to level 6.

Every earned level adds 2% training growth, capped at 10%. Match composure, decisions and teamwork receive up to +2; these bonuses affect match simulation without rewriting permanent player attributes. Each new level celebrates with +2 morale for the current team, capped at 100. Rewards persist with the manager and cannot be reclaimed by reloading. Newly met goals are recorded on date advancement. Youth minutes count completed matches at the managed club after this update; transferred players do not bring minutes earned elsewhere.

#### All 16 additions

1. Three rotating, consistent save backups and explicit recovery after a failed load. Restoring discards progress since that backup and preserves the failed save separately.
2. Android Back closes More; keyboard focus stays in the sheet and returns when it closes.
3. Faster daily training staff lookup, with the same underlying coaching formulas.
4. Transfer fee plus first-year wages and remaining weekly wage capacity before bidding.
5. Playing-time promise deadlines, remaining matches and injury pauses.
6. Side-by-side shortlisted player comparison, including ability, potential, wages and formation fit.
7. A six-month squad plan showing the coverage lost if expiring contracts are not renewed.
8. Saved monthly academy reviews, development changes and senior-promotion guidance.
9. Visible matchday substitutions and tactical shortcuts, with commands serialized against simulation steps.
10. A decisions inbox that keeps unresolved choices visible and puts urgent decisions first.
11. Individual monthly development goals measured against saved training reviews.
12. Loan monitoring for minutes, development, return dates and unused players.
13. Cash and wage forecasts over 4, 12 and 26 weeks, including agreed future registrations.
14. A persistent manager timeline for match totals, wins, trophies, promotions and anniversaries.
15. Stronger high contrast, saved reduced motion including charts, and mobile pinch zoom.
16. Manager achievements, XP, levels and capped team benefits in More.

Forecasts use current recurring cash flow and agreed registrations. Future match income, contract expiries and loan returns can change the result. Legacy milestone dates are labelled as earlier career progress rather than inventing exact dates.

## Download & Play

1. Visit the [latest release](https://github.com/TranquilMT/OpenFootManager-Android-ByTranquil/releases/latest) and download the **Android ARM64 APK**.
