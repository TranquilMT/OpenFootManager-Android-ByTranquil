# OFMtouch menu branding

The original OpenFootManager SVG remains unchanged. The menu adds the transparent `public/ofmtouch-wordmark.png` beneath it, while the collaboration screen retains the original upstream logo.

The TOUCH asset was generated with the built-in OpenAI image_gen tool on 2 October 2026 and integrated by TranquilMT. Prompt: typography-only uppercase TOUCH, bold wide squared geometric sports-game lettering, white faces with a crisp black outline and modest extruded edge, transparent background, no extra symbols or text. Original source output: `exec-8709ab9c-a748-4666-adcc-92afc96f2d82.png`. Dimensions: 2172 × 724; alpha preserved.

NIGHTLY is the build-channel identifier, intentionally retained in every locale. Its badge appears only when OFM_CHANNEL is nightly. The version/build stays separate at the right. Both footer elements share the same responsive row; source links sit above it, with safe-area spacing on mobile.
