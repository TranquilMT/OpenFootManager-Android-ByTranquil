# OFMtouch v0.7.5 — Matchday & Highlights

**Read the match. Make your move. Replay the moments that matter.**

## 🏟️ A clearer matchday

- ⚽ Follow both teams on one combined **11-v-11 tactical pitch**, with team identity, shirt numbers, substitutions and dismissals represented clearly.
- 🎙️ Read commentary that follows reached match events, with filters, follow-live scrolling and optional speech on supported devices.
- 📱 Use a compact scoreboard and persistent match controls, with cleaner layouts for portrait and landscape.

## 🎬 Recorded highlights & replays

- ✨ Choose **key highlights**, **extended highlights** or commentary-only viewing during and after matches.
- 🔎 Filter clips by club or goals, chances and incidents; browse oldest/newest first with mobile-friendly pagination.
- ⏯️ Play, pause, change replay pace, step actions or seek from the timeline and slider.
- 📋 Review an independent playlist with previous/next clips and optional automatic progression after the outcome.
- 📊 Follow historical scores, separate shootout tallies, named recorded participants and valid chance probability (xG).
- ♿ Use keyboard controls, focus restoration and reduced-motion support; replays hold live simulation, decisions and speech while open.

## 🧠 Match decisions & reliability

- 🔄 Open relevant substitution or tactical decisions from match alerts and review player performance during play and at full time.
- 🛡️ Recorded turnovers, missed chances, fouls, substitutions and tactical changes split replay sequences correctly.
- ⏸️ Pending match commands and manager phase transitions respect replay ownership and background visibility.
- 💾 Highlight mode preferences survive workspace visits, with safe fallback for invalid or blocked storage.
- 🌍 Updated controls and release highlights are translated into **12 languages**.

Recorded ball zones show event observations; the player layout shows tactical shape rather than continuous tracking.
