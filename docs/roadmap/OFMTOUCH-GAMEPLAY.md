# OFMtouch gameplay roadmap

Recovered from the agreed 27 September 2026 gameplay plan. This roadmap is separate from the upstream roadmap and Android-port checklist.

| Phase | Focus | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Dynamic Football World | Released |
| 2 | Transfer Market 2.0 | Released |
| 3 | Player Development 2.0 | Released |
| 4 | Youth Academies | Released |
| 5 | Manager Career | Released |
| 6 | Board & Dressing Room | Released through 0.6.9 |
| 7 | Match Engine Expansion | Released foundation; 0.7.4 AI/shootout/analysis integration |
| 8 | Mobile Match Centre | Partially integrated in 0.7.3–0.7.4; haptics/device validation remain |
| 9 | Mobile Inbox | Partially integrated; actions/filtering/unread controls ship; broader workflow audit remains |
| 10 | Android-Native Improvements | Partially integrated; predictive back/process-death replay/device validation remain |

Phase 7 covers contextual commentary, goal variations, woodwork, saves, defensive errors, tactical events, momentum/pressure, injuries, cards, substitution context, late pressure, comebacks, individual narratives, derby context, milestones and template leakage.

Phase 8 covers large touch tactical controls, mentality/tempo/pressing selectors, quick substitutions, a tactical drawer, event timeline, haptics and portrait testing. Phase 9 covers unified football messages, actionable offers and reports, alerts, filtering and unread indicators. Phase 10 covers navigation history, predictive back, resume/state recovery, rotating saves, keyboard/haptics, energy and low-end presets, scrolling and offline validation.

Current evidence and boundaries: [0.7.4 scope audit](0.7.4-approved-scope.md). Commit counts do not establish completion of the full roadmap.
